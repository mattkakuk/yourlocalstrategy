## **What Is Instagram Ads Manager?**

Instagram Ads Manager — officially known as Meta Ads Manager — is the platform
used to create, manage, and track paid campaigns on Instagram and Facebook. It
lives inside your Facebook Business account and provides a centralized
dashboard for your advertising.

For local businesses, this tool is invaluable. It allows you to laser-focus
your ads on specific zip codes — like **33410** , **33418** , and **33403** —
filtering by age, interests, and precise location. Instead of casting a wide
net, you put your budget directly in front of the local homeowners, families,
and professionals who matter most.

Meta Ads Manager offers several formats tailored to local business goals:

  * **Stories & Reels Ads:** Full-screen, vertical formats capturing high engagement. These work best for time-sensitive flash sales or authentic, short-form video content.

## **How to Set Up Your Campaign in 6 Steps**

### **1\. Define Your Campaign Objective**

Determine your goal: website traffic, lead form submissions, phone calls, or
brand awareness. For most local service businesses, **Traffic** or **Leads**
will deliver the best return on investment.

### **2\. Set Your Target Audience**

Narrow your geographic footprint to Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter, Juno Beach,
and North Palm Beach. Layer your targeting with specific behaviors or premium
lifestyle interests (e.g., country clubs or luxury home goods) to reach high-
income buyers. You can also upload your existing customer list to build a
**Custom Audience**.

### **3\. Choose Your Placements**

Meta recommends using **Advantage+ Placements** to maximize budget efficiency
across their network. However, if you have assets tailored specifically for
Instagram, choose manual placements to isolate the Instagram Feed, Stories,
and Reels.

### **4\. Set Your Budget and Schedule**

Start with a manageable daily budget. Even **$10 to $20 a day** can generate
meaningful results in a localized market. Alternatively, use a lifetime budget
with fixed start and end dates for time-sensitive promotions.

### **5\. Create Your Ad Creative**

Use bright, professional visuals reflecting the upscale Palm Beach Gardens
lifestyle. Write concise copy that addresses local consumer needs, and always
include a clear call to action (CTA) like _" Book a Free Consultation"_ or _"
Get a Quote."_

### **6\. Review, Launch, and Monitor**

Preview your ad on mobile before pushing it live. Once running, track key
metrics in Ads Manager—such as reach, click-through rate (CTR), and cost per
result—and tweak your creative based on performance data.

## **Tips to Maximize Local Results**

  * **Geotarget Aggressively:** Avoid wasting impressions on broad statewide targeting. Restrict your radius strictly to the neighborhoods where your ideal clients live.
  * **Design for Sound-Off Viewing:** Most users scroll without audio. Use clear text overlays and captions to ensure your message is communicated instantly.
  * **Retarget Warm Audiences:** Set up campaigns to re-engage people who have previously visited your website or interacted with your social profiles.
  * **Be Patient with the Learning Phase:** Meta 's algorithm needs **one to two weeks** to optimize delivery. Avoid turning off a campaign too early before it gathers sufficient data.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **How much do Instagram ads cost in Palm Beach Gardens?**

Costs depend on your industry and competition. On average, US businesses
experience a cost per click **(CPC) between $0.40 and $1.70** , and a cost per
thousand impressions **(CPM) between $6.00 and $14.00**. In a premium market
like Palm Beach Gardens, a starting budget of $10 to $30 per day is ideal for
testing.

### **Do I need a Facebook account to run Instagram ads?**

Yes. Instagram ads are managed through Meta Ads Manager, which requires a
Facebook Business account. You will need to link your Instagram profile to
your Facebook Business Page.

### **How do I target only local residents?**

In the audience section of Ads Manager, drop a pin on a map or input specific
zip codes (like 33410 and 33418) to isolate your delivery strictly to local
neighborhoods.

### **What ad type works best for service businesses?**

Video and Stories ads generally yield the highest engagement. Showing
authentic, local transformations builds trust quickly in visually driven
industries.
