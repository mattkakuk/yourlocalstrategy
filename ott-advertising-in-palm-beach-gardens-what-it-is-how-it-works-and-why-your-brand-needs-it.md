Viewers are cutting the cord and spending their screen time on streaming
platforms that cable never touched. For businesses in Palm Beach Gardens and
across South Florida, that shift is a real opportunity. OTT advertising —
Over-The-Top advertising — delivers video ads through internet-based streaming
services, bypassing traditional cable entirely.

U.S. OTT and Connected TV (CTV) ad spend surpassed $33 billion in 2025,
accounting for over 60% of total digital video ad investment. Within this
massive ecosystem, living-room Smart TVs capture the lion’s share of viewer
attention. Here's what you need to know to leverage this growth locally.

## What Is OTT Advertising?

OTT advertising delivers video ads through streaming platforms over the
internet — on smart TVs, phones, tablets, laptops, and streaming devices like
Roku or Fire TV. The "over-the-top" name refers to bypassing cable
infrastructure and delivering content directly to viewers.

OTT is often confused with Connected TV (CTV).

  *  **CTV** refers specifically to the device — a smart TV, gaming console, or streaming stick connected to the big screen.

  *  **OTT** is the broader delivery method — the content, platforms, and streaming ecosystem across all devices (including mobile and desktop).

Most campaigns run across both, but the distinction matters when building a
media plan or evaluating results.

## How OTT Advertising Works

OTT runs through a programmatic ecosystem. Instead of negotiating directly
with a TV network, advertisers set audience parameters, budgets, and goals —
and a Demand-Side Platform (DSP) handles placement automatically across
streaming apps in real time.

### The basic campaign flow:

  *  **Define your audience** — target by demographics, zip codes, viewing behavior, or your own first-party CRM data.

  *  **Create your ad** — typically a high-quality 15–30 second horizontal video.

  *  **Select platforms** — find the ad-supported streaming services where your audience spends time.

  *  **Buy inventory programmaticall** y — a DSP places ads across multiple platforms simultaneously.

  *  **Set targeting and frequency rules** — control who sees the ad, how often, and on which devices.

  *  **Launch, monitor, and optimize** — adjust creative and targeting rules while the campaign runs.

>  **The Key Advantage:** You are targeting specific audience segments and
households, not buying a rigid time slot and hoping the right people are
watching.

## Types of OTT Platforms and Ad Formats

Streaming inventory is divided into different viewing models:

  *  **AVOD (Advertising-Based Video on Demand):** Platforms like Tubi, Pluto TV, and YouTube are free for viewers and entirely funded by ads. This is where a large portion of local OTT advertising happens.

  *  **HVOD (Hybrid Video on Demand):** These are subscription-based services that offer cheaper, ad-supported tiers—such as Netflix, Disney+, and Hulu. These hybrid tiers now represent roughly 46% of subscriptions across major streamers.

  *  **TVOD / PVOD (Transactional/Premium VOD):** Pay-per-view models (like renting a movie on Apple TV) that offer highly limited direct ad space.

### Common Ad Formats

  *  **Pre-roll:** 10–30 seconds before content begins.

  *  **Mid-roll:** Inserted at natural commercial breaks; boasts incredibly high viewer completion rates.

  *  **Overlay and Banner:** Display elements that appear during playback without interrupting the video.

  *  **Interactive and Shoppable:** Viewers can click, scan an on-screen QR code, or act directly using their remote.

## Why OTT Works for Palm Beach Gardens Businesses

Local businesses have long been priced out of traditional broadcast TV. OTT
changes the game:

  *  **Hyper-Local Geographic Targeting:** Reach viewers in specific Palm Beach Gardens zip codes or neighborhoods within the West Palm Beach DMA — without paying to broadcast to the entire South Florida market.

  *  **Behavioral Targeting:** Put ads in front of affluent homeowners, golf enthusiasts, families, or any segment matching your exact customer profile.

  *  **Flexible Budgets:** Start small, test different video creatives, and scale up what performs.

  *  **Real-Time Measurement:** Deliver concrete completion rates, website visits, and digital conversions — data linear TV simply cannot provide.

## How Much Does OTT Advertising Cost?

Standard OTT inventory runs **$20–$50 CPM** (Cost Per Thousand impressions).
Premium placements on top-tier networks like Hulu or Roku range from
**$30–$100+ CPM** , depending on targeting precision, dayparting, and market
demand.

While tighter targeting costs more per impression, it dramatically reduces ad
waste. In local digital marketing, a higher CPM with precise targeting almost
always outperforms a cheap CPM with overly broad reach.

## OTT Advertising Best Practices

  *  **Hook viewers in the first 5 seconds:** Messaging needs to land fast in a non-skippable environment.

  *  **Use first-party data:** Uploading your own CRM email lists to target past customers or lookalikes outperforms generic demographic buys.

  *  **Set frequency caps:** Overexposure damages brand perception. Cap views so the same household doesn 't see your ad excessively.

  *  **Connect OTT to your funnel:** Follow up streaming exposure by retargeting those same households with display ads or paid search.

  *  **Test before scaling:** Run two creative versions simultaneously, find the clear winner, and shift your budget mid-flight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between OTT and CTV advertising?

OTT is the delivery method — streaming content over the internet across any
device (mobile, desktop, tablet, and TV). CTV is the specific device — the
actual Smart TV or streaming stick in the living room. CTV inventory generally
commands higher CPMs due to the full-screen, highly engaged viewing
environment.

### How much budget do I need to start?

Most self-serve DSP platforms allow local campaigns to start between
**$500–$2,000 per month** for initial testing. However, if you want a premium,
hands-on **Managed Service directly through Amazon Ads** , Amazon typically
requires a minimum spend of **$50,000**. Local businesses usually find the
most flexibility using self-serve DSPs or partnering with a local agency.

### Can OTT advertising drive local foot traffic?

Yes. Geographic and household-level targeting makes OTT incredibly effective
for brick-and-mortar businesses. Paired with location-based conversion
tracking, you can measure whether viewers who saw your streaming ad later
walked into your location — a powerful tool for restaurants, retail, and
service businesses in Palm Beach Gardens.

### What creative assets do I need for OTT ads?

OTT ads typically run 15 or 30 seconds long in a standard 16:9 horizontal
format. A clean, high-definition spot with a strong hook in the first five
seconds will outperform an overproduced ad with a buried message.

### How do I measure OTT campaign success?

Key metrics include Video Completion Rate (VCR), Cost Per Completed View
(CPCV), site visit lift, Cost Per Acquisition (CPA), and Return on Ad Spend
(ROAS). Many platforms offer verified digital footprint attribution, linking
CTV exposure directly to a user visiting your website or making a purchase.
