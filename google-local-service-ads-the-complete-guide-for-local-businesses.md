If you run a local service business in Palm Beach Gardens or anywhere in South
Florida, Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) may be the most cost-efficient lead
generation tool available to you. They sit at the very top of Google search
results, charge you only when someone contacts you, and come with built-in
trust signals that make new customers more likely to call. Here is everything
you need to know.

## What Are Google Local Service Ads?

LSAs are a pay-per-lead ad format built for local service businesses. Unlike
standard pay-per-click ads, you are only charged when a prospect actually
contacts you — by call or message. They appear above Google Ads and organic
results, making them the first thing searchers see.

Each eligible ad features the Google Verified Badge. Google streamlined its
badging system to this single, unified trust signal across all industries
(replacing the older "Google Guarantee" and "Google Screened" systems). To
earn this badge, businesses must pass Google's proprietary screening process,
which includes business registration, licensing verification, and background
checks where applicable.

>  **Note on Setup:** Google offers a  "Pre-Badge" ad status for select
categories. This allows your business to appear in search results and capture
leads at a lower ranking tier while your final onboarding checks are being
fully processed. _(Note: Pre-Badge ads are not available for healthcare,
locksmiths, or garage door services)._

## How Do LSAs Work?

The process is simple. A user searches for a local service — say, "pool
company Palm Beach County." Google checks their location and shows eligible
LSAs first. The user sees your ad with reviews, hours, and the Google Verified
Badge. If they call or message, you are charged for that lead. You manage
everything through the LSA dashboard or mobile app.

One key ranking factor is responsiveness. While your average response time is
heavily factored into Google's internal algorithm to determine your overall ad
position, opt-in message leads may also display estimated response times
(e.g., "Responds in a few minutes") directly on the ad. Fast response times
drastically improve both your ranking and consumer conversions.

## LSAs vs. Google Ads vs. Local SEO

Each channel serves a different role — and they work best together:

  *  **LSAs:** Pay-per-lead. Top-of-page placement. Google Verified Badge trust signal. Best for high-intent, ready-to-hire searchers.

  *  **Google Ads (PPC):** Pay-per-click. Broader keyword targeting. Faster setup with no background check. Best for wider search visibility.

  *  **Local SEO:** Free organic traffic. Long-term investment. Drives Map Pack and organic rankings. Best for sustainable growth.

The smartest local marketing strategy uses all three: LSAs for fast leads now,
PPC for reach, and SEO for long-term market dominance.

## Key Benefits of Google Local Service Ads

### Top-of-Page Placement

LSAs appear above everything — PPC ads, the Map Pack, and organic results.
That visibility alone drives significantly more clicks and calls.

### Pay Only for Real Leads

If someone sees your ad and scrolls past, you pay nothing. You are charged
only when a qualified prospect contacts you directly.

### Built-In Trust via the Google Verified Badge

For customers who do not know your brand yet, the Google Verified Badge
reduces friction. It signals that Google has thoroughly vetted your business
registration and professional credentials, instantly boosting customer
confidence.

### Flexible Budget Control

Set a weekly spending cap and pause your ads any time — useful when you are
fully booked. Google optimizes delivery within your budget automatically.

## How to Get Started

LSAs are available across dozens of local industries including home services,
legal, wellness, financial, healthcare, and real estate. Setup steps:

  1. Confirm your industry qualifies at Google's LSA help page.

  2. Create or connect your Google Business Profile with complete, accurate info.

  3. Sign up for LSAs and build your business profile.

  4. Submit your license, insurance, and business registration documents.

  5. Complete background checks for owners and applicable staff.

  6. Set your weekly budget, service area, and job types — then launch.

Once live, prioritize review collection. Reviews collected through your LSA
links will sync with your main Google Business Profile, directly lifting your
authority and performance across both Google Maps and the LSA network.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1\. How is the cost of LSAs determined?

You pay per lead — a call or message from a prospect. Cost per lead varies by
industry, location, and local competition. You control spending with a weekly
budget cap.

### 2\. What if I get a bad or irrelevant lead?

Google uses an Automated Lead Credits system to filter and credit back low-
quality leads (like spam or duplicate calls). Manual disputes are no longer
supported, and Google does not credit for "job type not serviced" or "geo not
serviced." To protect your budget, you must rate poor leads as "Very
Dissatisfied" within 30 days to train the machine-learning algorithm to stop
sending them, and tightly restrict your active job types in your dashboard.

### 3\. How long does approval take?

Typically a few days to a few weeks depending on your industry and how quickly
you submit documents. Eligible businesses can opt to run ads under a "Pre-
Badge" status while awaiting final background checks.

### 4\. Do LSAs work in Palm Beach County?

Yes. LSAs are available throughout Florida, including Palm Beach Gardens, West
Palm Beach, and surrounding areas. Home services, legal, wellness, real
estate, and financial services are among the eligible categories in this
market.

### 5\. Should I run LSAs if I already do SEO?

Absolutely. LSAs generate leads immediately while SEO builds long-term
visibility. The best-performing local businesses run both — LSAs for quick
wins, SEO for sustainable growth over time.
