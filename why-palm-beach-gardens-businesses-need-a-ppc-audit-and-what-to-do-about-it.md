## What Is a PPC Audit?

A PPC audit is a full diagnostic and health check of your pay-per-click
campaigns. It evaluates your account structure, ad asset quality, keyword
selection, landing page relevance, bid strategies, and tracking accuracy to
ensure every dollar is working efficiently.

Every campaign drifts over time. Search behavior changes, competitors adjust
their tactics, and platform algorithms update. Without a structured audit,
inefficiencies accumulate quietly until your cost-per-lead climbs and your ROI
shrinks. Industry data shows that a vast majority of small business accounts
go months without being looked at. Consistent, structured optimization is what
sets the top-performing advertisers apart from the rest.

## 5 Reasons Your PPC Campaign Needs an Audit

### 1\. Wasted spend on the wrong keywords

If your ads are triggering for searches that don't match your services, you're
paying for clicks that will never convert. An audit identifies which keywords
are draining your budget and where negative keywords need to be added to keep
targeting focused strictly on high-intent local searches.

### 2\. Broken conversion tracking

Faulty tracking is the most common issue found during a PPC audit. If
conversions aren't tracking correctly, your campaign is making automated
optimization decisions based on bad data. An audit verifies that your tags are
firing perfectly and that your most valuable local actions — calls, form
fills, and bookings — are being recorded accurately.

### 3\. Low Quality Scores hurting your placements

Google doesn't award top placements to the highest bidder alone. Quality Score
— measuring ad relevance, expected click-through rate (CTR), and landing page
experience — determines where your ads appear and what you pay per click. An
audit evaluates your setup so you can improve scores and lower your cost-per-
click.

### 4\. Bidding strategy misaligned with your goals

The wrong bidding strategy wastes money fast. Whether you are using manual
bidding or relying on Google's automated Smart Bidding, a PPC audit reviews
your setup to ensure the algorithm is properly optimizing for factors like
device performance, location data, and audience behavior — all of which matter
in a competitive local market like Palm Beach Gardens.

### 5\. Your competitors are constantly optimizing

While you're running the same campaign from six months ago, competitors may be
testing new offers and capturing keyword opportunities you're leaving open. A
PPC audit includes a competitive analysis to identify local market gaps you
can move into.

## What a PPC Audit Covers

A thorough audit examines every layer of your campaign:

  *  **Account Structure:** Campaigns should be organized cleanly by service type, location, or funnel stage. Brand and non-brand campaigns should always be separated.

  *  **Ad Copy & Creative:** An audit checks that your Responsive Search Ads (RSAs) feature diverse, high-quality headlines and descriptions, giving Google's machine learning the best possible assets to automatically test and serve.

  *  **Keyword Review:** We evaluate keyword match types, search term reports, and impression share — and build a robust negative keyword list to block irrelevant traffic.

  *  **Landing Page Alignment:** A high CTR paired with low conversions almost always points to a landing page problem. An audit identifies friction points on your pages that are costing you leads.

  *  **Location & Audience Targeting:** For Palm Beach Gardens businesses, geotargeting matters. An audit verifies campaigns are hyper-focused on the right ZIP codes and service areas so budget isn't wasted on locations you don't serve.

## How Often Should You Audit?

Audit your PPC campaigns two to four times per year at minimum. Beyond that
schedule, run an immediate audit any time your conversion rate drops
unexpectedly, you change your business strategy significantly, a major
platform update rolls out, or a aggressive new competitor enters the Palm
Beach County market.

### How much does a PPC audit cost in Palm Beach Gardens?

### How long does a PPC audit take?

A basic review takes a few hours, but a thorough account audit covering
keywords, conversion tracking, Quality Scores, landing pages, and competitor
benchmarking typically takes a few business days to fully analyze and
document.

### What is the difference between a PPC audit and ongoing management?

An audit is a diagnostic tool — it identifies exactly what is broken and
outlines the roadmap for what needs to change. Ongoing management is the
continuous day-to-day work of implementing those improvements, monitoring
daily performance, and adapting to real-time market shifts.

### Can I do a PPC audit myself?

You can review some of the basics, like scanning search terms for obvious
budget leaks or ensuring your phone number is correct. However, a professional
audit goes much deeper using specialized software, scripts, and historical
benchmarks. For competitive local markets, the cost of a professional audit is
almost always recovered instantly through the wasted ad spend it uncovers.

### How soon will I see results?

Immediate fixes like excluding irrelevant search terms or correcting broken
tracking can show an impact within days. Structural improvements to Quality
Scores and landing page conversion rates typically take a few weeks to mature.
Most local businesses see meaningful gains within 30 to 60 days of
implementing our audit recommendations.
